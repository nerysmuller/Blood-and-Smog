A City Built in Layers

Shroudkeep is inspired by the dense, irregular growth of late-Victorian London: old streets buried beneath newer roads, industrial corridors carved through poorer wards, bridges controlled as checkpoints, and wealth climbing physically away from the smoke. The map below is an unmarked period city plan used as Shroudkeep's base. Its fictional district names appear only on the interactive points layered above it.

Desolamus

Desolamus draws its silhouette and regional mood from historic Moldavia: river-cut borders, wooded interiors, old roads, mountain approaches, and settlements separated by long stretches of uneasy country.

What You Know

Shroudkeep is the capital and the center of industrial power. Most citizens know the country beyond it chiefly through freight routes, military reports, family stories, and rumor.
