What Shroudkeep Remembers

The official record claims four ancient houses rescued the region from famine, lawlessness, and plague. Their factories brought employment. Their banks brought order. Their hospitals brought relief.

Then why are their portraits unchanged?

Older records are less charitable. They describe blood registries, inherited contracts, locked private clinics, and servants required to present themselves at eastern gates after dark.

Blood & Hunger

Sunlight

Turning

Prohibited subject

Magic and the Sentinels

Arcane practice is forbidden. The Sentinels enforce the prohibition through raids, confiscation, imprisonment, and disappearance. Public records call this civic protection. Other records call it extermination.
