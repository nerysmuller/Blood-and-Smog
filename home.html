You've Escaped... Almost.

Guards in disarray in the Blackglass Gaol's central chamber. The smell of sulfer and putrid sludge, slushing beneath your feet. Just beyond the southern gate, a fellow prisoner's escape plan has been set in motion.

Shroudkeep has already decided what happens to people like you, but as rueful and cunning as you are, you've decided to change the story.

What Your Character Knows

The city

Shroudkeep, 1891

A sprawling industrial city divided by wealth, smoke, and old blood. The upper districts glitter beneath gaslight while the factory wards choke beneath their chimneys.

The law

Magic Is Forbidden

Arcane practice is treated as sedition. The Sentinels seize charms, books, relics, and anyone accused of using them. Proof is helpful, but not required.

The sickness

The Plague Spreads

Quarantines tighten. Doctors disagree. Entire streets disappear from public records. Wealth appears to be an excellent medicine.

The rulers

Old Families Endure

The oldest houses own factories, banks, hospitals, newspapers, and much of the city itself. Their influence is public. Their true appetites are discussed only in whispers.

The beginning

Blackglass Gaol

Your story begins inside the city's most infamous prison. You may know one another—or you may simply be the only people left alive when something goes terribly wrong.

The warning

Nothing Is Safe

This is a high-stakes gothic campaign. Alliances have costs, violence leaves scars, and survival may require choices that no clean hero would enjoy making.

The Shroudkeep Gazette

Hemlock Edition | December 4th, 1891

DISTURBANCE REPORTS AT BLACKGLASS GAOL

Officials have denied rumors of a coordinated escape attempt inside Blackglass Gaol, despite reports of magical disturbances, terrified guards, and one officer missing in the sludge pits. Witnesses claim several prisoners broke free of their cage and recovered their confiscated weapons while the western drawbridge began to lower. At press time, the prisoners remained inside the gaol. Reinforcements were approaching, and twelve seconds remained before the bridge reached the ground.
