Session Three • Too many questions. So little time.

Freedom with a cost.

Smogs gathers on the horizon as a new day dawns, the Hems thick with it. Wrecked from a night's worth of escape (and one of your lot down a limb), you've successfully escaped the Blackglass Gaol and are now on the run. Though, a potential ally's brought with her more questions than answers.

You know not what is in store for you, but the path ahead seems clear for now: you need rest. And the artificer? Needs to get back to his workshop.

What Your Character Knows

The city

Shroudkeep, 1891

A sprawling industrial city divided by wealth, smoke, and old blood. The upper districts glitter beneath gaslight while the factory wards choke beneath their chimneys.

The law

Magic Is Forbidden

Arcane practice is treated as sedition. The Sentinels seize charms, books, relics, and anyone accused of using them. Proof is helpful, but not required.

The sickness

The Plague Spreads

Quarantines tighten. Doctors disagree. Entire streets disappear from public records. Wealth appears to be an excellent medicine.

The rulers

Old Families Endure

The oldest houses own factories, banks, hospitals, newspapers, and much of the city itself. Their influence is public. Their true appetites are discussed only in whispers.

The beginning

Blackglass Gaol

Having escaped the city's most infamous prison, you've discovered this gaol is more than meets the eye. Perhaps deeper secrets lie beneath the naked eye.

The warning

Nothing Is Safe

This is a high-stakes gothic campaign. Alliances have costs, violence leaves scars, and survival may require choices that no clean hero would enjoy making.

The Shroudkeep Gazette

Hemlock Edition | December 4th, 1891

DISTURBANCE REPORTS AT BLACKGLASS GAOL

Officials have denied rumors of a coordinated escape attempt inside Blackglass Gaol, despite reports of magical disturbances, terrified guards, and one officer missing in the sludge pits. Witnesses claim several prisoners broke free of their cage and recovered their confiscated weapons while the western drawbridge began to lower. At press time, the prisoners remained inside the gaol. Reinforcements were approaching, and twelve seconds remained before things would reach a place of no return.

Hemlock Edition | December 5th, 1891

STRANGE NIGHTLY NOISES ROUSE DENIZENS OF THE HEMS

Amidst the disturbance reports at Blackglass Gaol, locals have reported instances of barking and howling late into the night and early morn. Old Town reporters on the scene claim Warden Severine Vey was unavailable for comment, as officials claim he has journeyed to Silk Street for "important business" and will be unavailable until further notice. In the meanwhile, seven prisoners have seemingly escaped Blackglass Gaol. If you or anyone knows of their whereabouts, please report your findings to your local enforcement. Sentinels, too, are on the lookout.
