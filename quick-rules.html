Beginner Field Guide

Field Guide • Beginner Copy

What You Can Do

You never need to know the exact rule before describing an attempt. Say what your character wants to do; the table will identify the roll.

On Your Turn

Move up to your Speed, take one Action, and use a Bonus Action when a feature grants one.

Reaction

A Reaction happens in response to a trigger, such as an opportunity attack.

Advantage

Roll two d20s and use the higher result.

Disadvantage

Roll two d20s and use the lower result.

Saving Throw

A defensive roll against danger, magic, poison, fear, or another effect.

Concentration

You can normally concentrate on only one concentration spell at a time.

Combat Shortcut

Know your AC, maximum HP, spell save DC, and two or three attacks you use most often.

Table Rule

Ask questions. Beginners are not expected to memorize a rulebook before being allowed to have fun.
